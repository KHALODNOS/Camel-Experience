## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a warm, sun-drenched, editorial luxury aesthetic tailored to modern international travelers seeking authentic Moroccan coastal and desert adventures. The visual tone bridges traditional Moroccan warmth—sun-baked clay, golden afternoon light, and handcrafted intimacy—with contemporary European boutique travel design: generous negative space, sophisticated serif typography, and tactile surfaces.

The emotional response is welcoming, grounded, adventurous, and distinctly refined. Visual treatments avoid tourist cliches or gaudy resort tropes, opting instead for organic textures, atmospheric lighting, and architectural gestures derived from Moroccan arches, zellij geometries, and riad courtyards.

## Colors
The palette is built from the mineral and earth pigments of the Souss-Massa landscape:

- **Primary (`#C26A18` / Ochre Amber):** Anchors primary call-to-actions, badges, and active navigation indicators. Reflects the glow of Agadir’s golden-hour dunes.
- **Secondary (`#9C411E` / Sun-baked Terracotta):** Used for supporting accents, secondary interactive treatments, and high-emphasis hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` / Golden Sunset):** Reserved for star ratings, special inclusions, and subtle warm glow borders.
- **Canvas & Backgrounds:** The base canvas uses Sand Beige (`#FAF6F0`) with layered sections in Warm Cream (`#F4ECE1`).
- **Surfaces & Cards:** Elevated elements sit on Pure Warm Ivory (`#FFFDF9`) framed by subtle sand borders (`#E6DAC8`).
- **Typography & High Contrast:** Primary copy uses Deep Charcoal (`#1C1917`) and Espresso (`#292524`), with secondary descriptive copy in Warm Earth Gray (`#78716C`).
- **Color Discipline:** Vibrant blues and synthetic saturated hues are avoided across all interface elements, allowing ocean and sky blues to appear strictly through authentic location photography.

## Typography
The system uses an intentional pairing between **Playfair Display** (editorial, classical, artisanal) and **Plus Jakarta Sans** (humanist, legible, functional). 

- Editorial headlines evoke the timeless romance of North African travel narratives. Italic variants of Playfair Display should be used sparingly for poetic qualifiers (e.g., *at Sunset*, *Traditional Mint Tea*).
- All transactional elements, scheduling details, tour specs, pricing, and form inputs strictly use Plus Jakarta Sans to maintain effortless scanning for international travelers on mobile screens.
- Numerical badges and prices leverage bold weights with tightened tracking for instant clarity.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a responsive 12-column grid on desktop (max content container width: 1280px) and a single-column reflow on mobile devices with generous outer breathing room.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with 1.5rem gutters and 4rem outer canvas margins. Ample vertical spacing (space-2xl to 6rem) separates thematic tour experiences.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 1.5rem gutters and 2rem outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 1rem gutters and 1.25rem margins. Cards and booking controls reflow to edge-aligned vertical stacks or horizontally swipeable carousels with peek margins.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth mimics natural, directional Saharan sunlight rather than cold synthetic drop shadows:

- **Surface Layering:** Elevated content cards sit on Pure Warm Ivory (`#FFFDF9`) bordered with a fine, 1px warm sand outline (`#E6DAC8`).
- **Warm Ambient Shadow:** Shadows incorporate a warm umber tint:
  - *Subtle (Cards & Containers):* `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04);`
  - *Floating Interactive (Hover & Modals):* `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(194, 106, 24, 0.16), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.06);`
- **Atmospheric Image Overlays:** Imagery containers apply a delicate gradient overlay from bottom to top: `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(28, 25, 23, 0) 40%, rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.65) 100%)` ensuring high contrast for overlaid text while retaining golden warmth.

## Shapes
The base geometric radius is set to level 2 (0.5rem base, with larger components scaling to 1rem and 1.5rem / `rounded-2xl`). 

- **Cards & Visual Containers:** Use 1rem to 1.5rem (`rounded-2xl`) corners to soften layout boundaries and invoke sculpted clay or tadelakt plasterwork.
- **Moroccan Arch Gestures:** Hero media frames and selective promotional containers can feature asymmetric rounding (e.g., top-left and top-right radii set to `rounded-t-full` or large pill arcs) to echo traditional horseshoe and keyhole arches.
- **Buttons & Tags:** Form controls and CTA buttons use generous pill-style rounding (9999px) or refined 0.75rem rounded corners for ergonomic touch interactions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Booking CTA:** Filled with Primary Warm Ochre (`#C26A18`), bold Plus Jakarta Sans text in Pure Ivory (`#FFFDF9`), rounded to 9999px or `1rem`. On hover, transitions to Clay (`#9C411E`) with a soft Ochre ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -2px rgba(194, 106, 24, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / WhatsApp Action:** Outlined border in `#C26A18` with 1.5px stroke, Warm Ivory background, and Espresso text. Hover introduces a subtle warm sand wash (`#F4ECE1`).

### Pricing Badges
- High-visibility pill indicators (e.g., `From €10` / `€15 / Person`).
- Encased in a Warm Cream background (`#F4ECE1`) with a solid Ochre accent border or an inverted Ochre badge with white typography for featured sunset packages.

### Experience Cards
- Built with a `#FFFDF9` surface, 1px `#E6DAC8` border, and `rounded-2xl` corners.
- Features a 16:10 or 4:3 photography header with rounded top edges, duration tags (e.g., "2 Hours • Sunset"), pickup inclusions, clear pricing hierarchy, and an integrated booking trigger.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFDF9` with an `#E6DAC8` stroke and 0.75rem corner radius.
- Focus state: Replaces outline with a 2px `#C26A18` ring and subtle warm inner glow. Label text is set in Espresso (`#292524`).

### Chips & Inclusions
- Compact pill tags denoting experience perks (e.g., "Hotel Transfer Included", "Moroccan Mint Tea", "Beach & Dunes"). Styled with Sand Beige fill (`#F4ECE1`), Charcoal text, and no heavy border.
